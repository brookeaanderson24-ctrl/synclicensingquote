# Sync Quote Request: User Manual

This guide is for music supervisors, filmmakers, advertising agencies and anyone else who needs to ask a music publisher for a sync license quote.

## What the app does for you

Publishers need the same handful of details before they can quote a sync license. This app collects them in one form, flags what is missing, and produces a one-page PDF you can email to the publisher. That saves the back-and-forth emails asking for term, territory, media and so on.

It does **not** give you a price, and it does not mean any rights are cleared. The publisher decides the fee and the terms. The app covers the composition only. If you want to use an existing recording, you must also ask the recording owner for a separate master use license.

## Quick start

1. Open the app link.
2. Fill in the form. Required fields are marked **Required**. Recommended fields are marked **Recommended**. The rest are **Optional**.
3. Watch the one-page preview fill in as you type. On a phone, use the **Preview** tab at the top to see it.
4. Check the "Items for publisher to confirm" list. Fill in what you can.
5. When all required fields are done, select **Download PDF**.
6. Attach the PDF to an email and send it to the publisher yourself. The app cannot send email.

Want to see how it works first? Select **Load example**. It fills the form with fictional entries. Select **Clear form** when you are done.

## Understanding the messages

- **Errors (red)** appear under a field after you leave it, or when you select the "required fields remaining" link at the top. Download PDF and Print stay off until there are none.
- **Warnings (amber)** are listed in "Items for publisher to confirm". They do not block the PDF. They print on the PDF so the publisher can see what is still open. Some are questions, such as "Term is perpetual but media is limited. Please confirm the intended scope." They are not judgments, and none of them is about price.
- **Notes (gray)** are for your information and appear on screen only.

The full list of messages is on the **How this works** page, with the exact wording and what triggers each one.

## Tips

- Add the publisher's name if you know it. It speeds up routing.
- If you do not have a budget yet, tick "No budget set yet" instead of leaving it blank. That answers the publisher's question.
- Keep the scene description short and concrete: what happens on screen and how the music is heard.
- The PDF holds one page. Descriptions are limited to 300 characters each for that reason.

## Troubleshooting

**Download PDF is gray.** Either a required field is missing (check the "required fields remaining" link) or the page could not load its PDF library. Check your internet connection and reload.

**A "?" shows up in the PDF.** The PDF font supports Latin letters and accents only. Characters from other scripts, such as Japanese or Cyrillic, print as "?". The app shows a warning listing them.

**Nothing downloaded after I selected Download PDF.** Check your browser's download settings and any blocked-download notice. On some phones the PDF opens in a viewer instead of saving. Use the share or save option there.

**Copy summary did not copy.** Some browsers block automatic copying. The app then shows a box with the text. Select it and copy it by hand.

**My entries disappeared.** The app does not save anything. Refreshing or closing the page clears the form. Download the PDF before you leave.

## Your privacy

Everything you type stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server and nothing is stored. The page loads one PDF library from a public content delivery network when it opens, and your entries are not sent there.
